# AI Patro — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** June 20, 2026

AI Patro ("we", "our", or "us") operates the AI Patro mobile application (the
"App"). This page explains our policies regarding the collection, use and
disclosure of personal data when you use the App.

## Information we do not collect

AI Patro does **not** collect, store or transmit any personal information. The
App works entirely offline. No account registration is required.

## Data stored on your device

The App stores only your selected calendar date locally on your device using
Flutter's standard state management. This data never leaves your device.

## Internet permission

The App declares the `INTERNET` permission in its manifest to support potential
future features. As of the current version, no network requests are made.

## Third-party services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs or tracking
libraries.

## Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from
children under 13. The App is suitable for all ages.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on
this page with an updated date.
