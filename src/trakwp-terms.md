## What the numbers mean

The six ranking multipliers follow WordPress.org's published formula. A predicted position also depends on text relevance, which can only be estimated, so predicted ranks are estimates. A fit score on a keyword says how close that estimate is to the live results.

Downloads are not new users. WordPress.org counts updates as downloads. Active installs are the rounded bands WordPress.org publishes.

Rank and install history start the day a listing is first snapshotted. Official daily downloads can go back about a year, because WordPress.org publishes those.
