NepTek Publisher — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

NepTek Publisher is a private tool used only by the administrator of the NepTek Facebook Page (facebook.com/NepTekOfficial) and its linked Instagram account (@neptekofficial). It publishes the Page's own videos and images, posts the Page's own comments on them, and reads the Page's own statistics.

The short version: the app is not offered to the public. Nobody else can log in to it, and it does not collect personal data from people who watch or follow NepTek.

What the app does

Using Meta's Graph API, on behalf of the NepTek Page only, the app:

  • Uploads and schedules NepTek's own reels and photo posts
  • Posts a comment from the Page on those posts (a question and the sources)
  • Reads the Page's own insights (plays, watch time, reach, follows)
  • Reads comments left on NepTek's posts so the Page administrator can reply

Data it stores

  • An access token for the NepTek Page, stored only on the administrator's own

computer and never shared

  • A local log of what the Page published (post IDs, times, statistics)

The app does not store, sell or share information about the people who view, like or comment on NepTek's posts. Comments are read only through Meta's own systems so they can be answered, and are not copied into any other database.

Data deletion

There is no user data to delete, because no one other than the Page administrator uses the app. If you believe the app holds any data about you, email the address below and it will be deleted.
