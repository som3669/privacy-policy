Privacy policies

Policies for my browser extensions and apps. None of them collect, transmit or sell personal data — each page states exactly what its product reads and what it stores on your own device.

Adwipe Chrome extension — Blocks ads, trackers, cookie nags and malware. No data collection. AutoFiller Chrome extension — Fills web forms in one click from a local profile. No passwords, no servers. Hookrate Chrome extension — YouTube channel and video research shown on the pages you already visit. No server. TabRest Chrome extension — Auto-suspends idle tabs to save CPU and memory. Everything stays on your device. AI Patro Android app — Nepali calendar and panchang. Works fully offline, collects nothing.
